Do not place this image anywhere on the website. Do not add it to the header, nav, hero, footer, buttons, or any page section. Do not replace or restyle any logo that is already visible in the page layout. The only job is the browser icon and the icon Google shows in search results.

- `<link rel="icon" type="image/png" sizes="48x48" href="/favicon-48x48.png">`
- `<link rel="apple-touch-icon" href="/apple-touch-icon.png">`
- `<link rel="manifest" href="/site.webmanifest">`
