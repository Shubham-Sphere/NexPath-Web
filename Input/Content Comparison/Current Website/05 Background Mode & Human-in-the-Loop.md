# 5. Background Mode & Human-in-the-Loop Development — CURRENT

- **Eyebrow:** MORE PRODUCTIVE DEVELOPMENT
- **Title:** BACKGROUND MODE & HUMAN-IN-THE-LOOP DEVELOPMENT
- **Heading:** Let suitable work run in the background. Step in when your judgment matters.
- **Intro:** Not every development task needs your attention from beginning to end. Testing, verification, and other suitable follow-up work can take time, even when they do not need continuous supervision.
- **Intro 2:** Start background mode when you want a suitable task to continue while you focus on other work, or let NexPath surface the option when a task is ready for background verification. Work can make progress while you are waiting on a coding agent or using time when a subscription would otherwise be idle.

## Subsections (2 cards)

1. **Save time and optimize token costs**
   For suitable tasks, NexPath can use a more cost-effective coding agent or a configured local model instead of routing every step through a premium model. This can reduce unnecessary token costs while the primary coding agent remains available for work that needs it.
   The agent and model should still fit the task. Cost optimization does not mean accepting weaker verification or compromising the quality required for the work.

2. **Return to the loop when it matters**
   Background work does not remove you from important decisions. When a task needs clarification, approval, or engineering judgment, NexPath brings you back into the loop.
   You remain the decision-maker. The aim is to save waiting time and reduce repetitive coordination while keeping you involved where your judgment makes a difference.

- **Supporting line:** Spend less time waiting. Reduce unnecessary token costs. Stay involved where your judgment matters.
